How this is calculated: the projection grows your savings at the real return, (1 + return) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1, compounded monthly, so every figure is in today's dollars and is directly comparable to your FIRE number. It assumes your monthly savings rise with inflation, returns are constant every year, and there are no taxes or fees. Real markets are uneven, which is why the sensitivity range matters more than any single estimate.

This calculator is provided for general educational purposes only. It uses simplified, generic assumptions and model allocations — it is not personalized financial, investment, or tax advice, and it does not take the place of guidance from a licensed advisor. Actual returns, timelines, and suitable allocations vary by individual circumstances.
